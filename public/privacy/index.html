DATA PROTECTION & PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Our transparent privacy standards, zero-tracking commitment, and adherence to the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the EU GDPR, and the Data Protection Act 2018.

Audio Analyser operates on a strict privacy-first foundation: we do not collect personal tracking data, we do not employ third-party advertising cookies, and we do not sell or monetize user information.

1. Data Controller & Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the website audioanalyser.github.io, documentation portals, and associated open-source tools provided by Audio Analyser. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, the Data Controller is the Audio Analyser Open Source Project maintainer, Sébastien Rousseau.

2. Zero-Tracking & Cookie Policy

We believe in privacy by design. Our website and documentation adhere to the following principles:

  • No Tracking Cookies: We do not place advertising, retargeting, or behavioral profiling cookies on your browser.
  • No Third-Party Analytics Trackers: We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.
  • Local Preference Storage: We use browser localStorage solely to remember your chosen theme preference (light or dark mode). This data never leaves your device.
  • No Cookie Banner Required: Because we only use strictly necessary technical storage that is privacy-neutral, no intrusive cookie consent banner is needed.

3. Information We May Process

We only process personal data when you explicitly and voluntarily provide it to us:

  • Support Inquiries: When you submit a message through our developer contact form, we collect your name, email address, subject, and the message content to respond to your technical request.
  • Server Access Logs: Like all web infrastructure, the hosting platform (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare) may temporarily process technical connection data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and server diagnostics.
  • Audio Data Processing Notice: Audio Analyser's client-side WebAudio algorithms and offline Python packages process audio data locally on your device or self-hosted server. Audio recordings are never transmitted to our servers without your explicit architecture configuration.

4. Lawful Basis for Processing (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Under Article 6 of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, our processing of personal data is governed by the following lawful bases:

  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): When you voluntarily submit an inquiry through our contact form.
  • Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): Processing necessary technical server logs to ensure server integrity, security defense, and open-source documentation delivery.

5. Data Retention & Security Standards

We apply robust technical measures to protect any data handled:

  • Retention Policy: Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to resolve the inquiry and are permanently deleted after 180 days.
  • End-to-End Encryption: All website traffic and contact form submissions are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.
  • Subresource Integrity (SRI): All scripts and stylesheets are cryptographically hashed using SHA-384 to guarantee tamper-proof execution.
  • Content Security Policy (CSP): Strict CSP headers enforce authorized script execution and prevent unauthorized external connections.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Under UK and EU data protection regulations, you possess comprehensive rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of Access: You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can request deletion of your personal data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can request restriction of data processing under certain conditions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request transfer of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object: You can object at any time to the processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please submit a request via our Contact Form. We will respond within 30 calendar days without charge.
