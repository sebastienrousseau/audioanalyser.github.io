      <article class="card stack" style="padding:var(--gap);">
        <h2>2. Zero-Tracking &amp; Cookie Policy</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>We believe in privacy by design. Our website and documentation adhere to the following principles:</p>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>No Tracking Cookies:</strong> We do not place advertising, retargeting, or behavioral profiling cookies on your browser.</li>
            <li><strong>No Third-Party Analytics Trackers:</strong> We do not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixels, or cross-site tracking scripts.</li>
            <li><strong>Local Preference Storage:</strong> We use browser <code>localStorage</code> solely to remember your chosen theme preference (light or dark mode). This data never leaves your device.</li>
            <li><strong>No Cookie Banner Required:</strong> Because we only use strictly necessary technical storage that is privacy-neutral, no intrusive cookie consent banner is needed.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </article>

      <!-- 3. Information We May Collect -->
      <article class="card stack" style="padding:var(--gap);">
        <h2>3. Information We May Process</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>We only process personal data when you explicitly and voluntarily provide it to us:</p>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Support Inquiries:</strong> When you submit a message through our <a href="/contact/index.html">developer contact form</a>, we collect your name, email address, subject, and the message content to respond to your technical request.</li>
            <li><strong>Server Access Logs:</strong> Like all web infrastructure, the hosting platform (GitHub Pages / Cloudflare) may temporarily process technical connection data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for security monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and server diagnostics.</li>
            <li><strong>Audio Data Processing Notice:</strong> Audio Analyser's client-side WebAudio algorithms and offline Python packages process audio data locally on your device or self-hosted server. Audio recordings are never transmitted to our servers without your explicit architecture configuration.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>

            <li><strong>Legitimate Interests (Art. 6(1)(f)):</strong> Processing necessary technical server logs to ensure server integrity, security defense, and open-source documentation delivery.</li>
          </ul>

        <h2>5. Data Retention &amp; Security Standards</h2>
        <div class="prose">
          <p>We apply robust technical measures to protect any data handled:</p>
          <ul>
            <li><strong>Retention Policy:</strong> Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to resolve the inquiry and are permanently deleted after 180 days.</li>
            <li><strong>End-to-End Encryption:</strong> All website traffic and contact form submissions are transmitted over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections.</li>
            <li><strong>Subresource Integrity (SRI):</strong> All scripts and stylesheets are cryptographically hashed using SHA-384 to guarantee tamper-proof execution.</li>
            <li><strong>Content Security Policy (CSP):</strong> Strict CSP headers enforce authorized script execution and prevent unauthorized external connections.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
